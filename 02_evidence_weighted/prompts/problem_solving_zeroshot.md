As an interview evaluator, your task is to assess and rate the levels of 'problem solving' exhibited by applicants based on their interview responses. 'problem solving' is defined as: 'This competency refers to the ability to identify, analyze, determine causative factors, and find solutions for problems. Those ranking high in this competency are able to isolate the issue and use appropriate techniques to resolve the situation.'. These are some key behavioral categories of 'problem solving': 'Recognizes and addresses issues, anticipates problems, identifies appropriate solutions, and solves problems.'.

The following is a Behaviorally Anchored Rating Scale which describes each of the behavioral categories at the rating levels of 1, 3, and 5 on ‘Problem Solving’.

For ‘Recognizes and Addresses Issues’, an applicant that should scored a 1 on ‘Problem Solving’: Ignores or fails to recognize, define, or address issues as they arise. An applicant that should scored a 3 on ‘Problem Solving’: Able to recognize, identify, and analyze problems by utilizing available information. Able to develop a basic understanding of the problem. An applicant that should scored a 5 on ‘Problem Solving’: Able to proactively recognize, identify, and analyze problems by utilizing available information. Understands how this information relates to the problem and is able to create a detailed response plan.

For ‘Anticipates Problems’, an applicant that should scored a 1 on ‘Problem Solving’: Fails to anticipate the development of problems. An applicant that should scored a 3 on ‘Problem Solving’: Anticipates problems and effectively analyzes them to determine a resolution. An applicant that should scored a 5 on ‘Problem Solving’: Anticipates problems and proactively develops alternatives in advance to prevent problems from arising.

For ‘Identifies Appropriate Solutions’, an applicant that should scored a 1 on ‘Problem Solving’: Unable to develop problem solving strategies. An applicant that should scored a 3 on ‘Problem Solving’: Thinks critically about the advantages and disadvantages of proposed solutions. An applicant that should scored a 5 on ‘Problem Solving’: Expertly assesses the downstream impacts of proposed resolutions to determine the optimal solution.

For ‘Solves Problems’, an applicant that should scored a 1 on ‘Problem Solving’: Fails to seek and act upon appropriate measures for the resolution of the problem. An applicant that should scored a 3 on ‘Problem Solving’: Resolves problems effectively. An applicant that should scored a 5 on ‘Problem Solving’: Resolves problems effectively and develops a plan to avoid similar situations in the future.

You receive the question one applicant was asked and the applicant's full response.

Based on the information provided, rate the response on a scale of 1 to 5 (where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest) in demonstrating 'problem solving'. Output your rating as a single number without any commentary on the reasoning for your rating. If the response shows no behaviour of 'problem solving' that can be placed on this scale, output 0 (not estimable).

Output field: `manifestation`, 0 to 5.
